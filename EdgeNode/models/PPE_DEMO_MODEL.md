# PPE demo model

`ppe_demo.pt` is the YOLOv8n demo checkpoint from
`ayushgupta7777/safetyvision-yolov8` on Hugging Face.

- Source: https://huggingface.co/ayushgupta7777/safetyvision-yolov8
- License: AGPL-3.0
- Classes used here: Person, Mask, NO-Mask, Gloves, NO-Gloves

This public model is suitable for demonstration and human-reviewed alerts. It
has not been validated on the client's camera, gloves, lighting, distance, or
site and must not be treated as production safety enforcement without local
testing and retraining.
